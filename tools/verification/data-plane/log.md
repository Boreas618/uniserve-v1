# Predictions

Each entry is written before the run it names and is not edited afterwards; the outcome is added beneath it. A run that times out or stops at a bound is INCONCLUSIVE. Runs are on rank-1 (144 cores, 956 GB) with TLC 1.8.0 under JDK 21; TLC's states lie in `artifacts/verification/data-plane/` and are removed when a run ends.

## 1. One handoff between two nodes (`MC_Handoff`), 2026-11-18

- **Model:** `KvPlane` with one request (one full page, a tail) routed from a prefill replica on one node to a decode replica on another, two device pages per replica, one host page per node, one seed, no switch, three tiering moves; all invariants of `PlaneSafety` and `ConfigUntouched`.
- **Prediction:** none was written before this run, the model's first; it is recorded as it happened.
- **Outcome:** `Holding` is violated in seven states: the handoff chooses to push both items, the prefill releases its pages before either push has started (the rule waited only for pushes in flight), and the push that then starts reads a page the prefill no longer holds.
- **Consequence:** the prefill keeps its pages until every push the handoff chose has ended: `Release` waits while a push is in flight or an item chosen for a push still waits for it (`Pushing`). The implementation's counterpart is the prefill's release after the handoff's completion, not after the pushes it has issued (`specs/kv-data-plane.md` §4).

## 4. Reachability and hazards of one handoff (`MC_Handoff`), 2026-11-18

- **Model:** as entry 2. Witnesses (`PlaneWitness`, each checked alone with `TypeOK`, each a behaviour the design must be able to reach): `NoPush`, `NoRead`, `NoPullHost`, `NoPullStore`, `NoUnkeyedRead`, `NoStaleMiss`, `NoExpiredRead`. Hazards, each switched on alone with every invariant: `EarlyRelease`, `UnreservedPush`, `KeyedTail`; and `KeyedTail` with `Filled` and `Faithful` alone.
- **Prediction:** every witness is violated: a full page delivered by a push, by a read, by a pull from the decode node's host tier (the page put into the store from the prefill's device, fetched into the decode node's host page, the node reported), by a pull from the store; the tail delivered by a read; a pull the directory showed that then misses (the host page evicted or the store's object removed after the report) and is computed; a read whose lease expired before its acknowledgement, computed. `EarlyRelease` violates `LeaseHeld` (the prefill releases its tail's page while the read's lease holds it); `UnreservedPush` violates `Reserved` (the decode aborts with a push in flight and frees the page it writes); `KeyedTail` violates `UnkeyedPushed` (the tail is pulled from the store by the full page's key), and with `Filled` and `Faithful` alone violates `Filled` (the decode's tail page receives the full page's content).
- **Outcome:** as predicted. The first launch stopped before TLC started (a log directory given relative to the folder the runner leaves) and was run again unchanged. Witnesses, each violated, with the length of its shortest behaviour and the distinct states searched: `NoPush` 10 states (2,284), `NoRead` 11 (7,134), `NoUnkeyedRead` 11 (7,372), `NoExpiredRead` 13 (18,298), `NoPullStore` 13 (35,447), `NoStaleMiss` 14 (57,135), `NoPullHost` 15 (79,589). Hazards: `EarlyRelease` violates `LeaseHeld` in six states (`Route`, `Compute`, `Reserve`, `Choose` of a read, `Release`); `UnreservedPush` violates `Reserved` in seven (`Choose` of a push, `StartPush`, `Abort`); `KeyedTail` violates `UnkeyedPushed` in eight (`Choose` of a pull from the store, the full page put into the store, the events, `StartGet` of the tail), and with `Filled` and `Faithful` alone violates `Filled` in nine, the get's end writing the full page's content into the tail's page.

## 5. Finality of one handoff (`MC_Finality`), 2026-11-18

- **Model:** the configuration of `MC_Handoff` with no symmetry, under `FairSpec`: weak fairness of each request's own steps (a lease's expiry among them) and of the ends of moves; the environment, cancellation, eviction, moves started and reports have none. Properties `Finality` (a routed request ends) and `Released` (an ended request's prefill pages are released), with every invariant of `PlaneSafety`.
- **Prediction:** both hold, no invariant is violated: whatever the directory states when the handoff chooses, a pull that misses is computed by the decode, a read whose lease expired is computed, and the prefill releases once its pushes ended and its leases ended. The distinct states are those of `MC_Handoff` times at most the two orders of its two device pages per replica (under a million).
- **Outcome:** as predicted for the properties, not for the size. The search completed with `Finality` and `Released` holding and no invariant violated: 11,979,327 states generated, 1,726,447 distinct, depth 32, in 23 min 33 s on 8 workers (the temporal check most of it), beside the runs of entries 3, 6 and 7. The states are four times those of `MC_Handoff` (3.99), not two: `MC_Handoff`'s symmetry permutes the two device pages of each of the two replicas, a group of four, and the prediction counted one replica's.

## 6. Hazards and witnesses of reuse and switches (`MC_Reuse`, `MC_Stale`, `MC_Switch`), 2026-11-18

- **Model:** as entry 2, run beside the searches of entry 3. Hazards with every invariant: `Unfenced` on `MC_Reuse`, `StaleAddress` on `MC_Stale`, `SeedBlind`, `SeedlessKey` and `RoleFlush` on `MC_Switch`. Hazards with `Filled` and `Faithful` alone: `EarlyRelease` and `UnreservedPush` on `MC_Reuse` (on `MC_Handoff` no second request rewrites the released pages, so no wrong content is read there), `RoleFlush` on `MC_Switch`. Witnesses alone: `NoReuse` on `MC_Reuse`, `NoSeedMix` and `NoRoleDrain` on `MC_Switch`.
- **Prediction:** `Unfenced` violates `Filled`: the first request's tail is read under a lease that expires, the prefill releases the page, the second request's prefill writes its own tail into it, the read ends and its acknowledgement takes it. `StaleAddress` violates `Integrity` (a move copies a device or host page the directory names under one key after it came to hold the other, and the store or the host tier caches it under the first) or `Filled` (a pull copies such a host page). `SeedBlind` violates `Filled`: the prefill's replica switches seed, a request is routed across the two seeds, and its pushed page holds the other seed's content. `SeedlessKey` violates `Faithful`: the first request's page is cached under the seedless key, the prefill's replica switches seed, the second request's prefill reuses that page. `RoleFlush` violates `Holding`, `Reserved` or `LeaseHeld`: the decode's replica switches role while it holds a request's reserved pages. Alone with `Filled` and `Faithful`: `EarlyRelease` violates `Filled` (the leased tail page released, then rewritten by the second request before the read ends), `UnreservedPush` violates `Filled` (the first request aborts with a push in flight, the second's reservation takes the page and is delivered, then the first push overwrites it), `RoleFlush` violates `Filled` (the flushed page reserved again by the second request and written while the first still holds it as delivered). Every witness is violated.
- **Outcome:** every property predicted is the one reported; two behaviours differ from those predicted. `Unfenced` violates `Filled` in thirteen states (the leases of both of the first request's pages expire, the tail's read starts, the prefill releases, the second request's prefill writes its tail into the released page, the read ends and is acknowledged); `StaleAddress` violates `Integrity` in eleven (the first request's page is reported on the device and released, a move takes the reported device address, the page is evicted and the second request's prefill writes its own prefix there, the move's end caches that content under the first key); `SeedBlind` violates `Filled` in eight (`EpochSwitch` of the prefill's replica before the route, the push delivers the other seed's content); `SeedlessKey` violates `Faithful` in six (the second request's prefill reuses the page of the other seed). `RoleFlush` violates `Holding` in four states, but on the prefill's replica, not the decode's as predicted: a role switch of the prefill's replica after `Compute` frees the pages it holds for the request. With `Filled` and `Faithful` alone: `EarlyRelease` violates `Filled` in eleven states, `UnreservedPush` in fourteen (the aborted request's push ends over the second request's delivered tail), as predicted; `RoleFlush` in ten, again on the prefill's side (the flushed tail page is written by the second request's prefill and then pushed as the first request's tail), not by a second reservation on the decode. Witnesses: `NoReuse` in seven states, `NoSeedMix` in nine (both items refused across the seeds and computed by the decode), `NoRoleDrain` in eleven (the decode's replica switched to prefill after the request decodes). Each search stopped at its violation within 343,741 states generated.

## 7. Reuse, a stale directory and switches with fewer tiers (`MC_Reuse`, `MC_Stale`, `MC_Switch`), 2026-11-18

- **Model:** as entry 2, run beside the searches of entry 3, which at ten minutes stood at 57, 64 and 55 million distinct states at depths 28, 24 and 22 with their queues still growing. Each configuration keeps what it was made for with fewer tiers: `MC_Reuse` with no host tier and one move (a put into the store), so its pulls are from the store; `MC_Stale` with full pages alone and two device pages per replica (the unkeyed items never meet the directory), its one host page and two moves kept; `MC_Switch` with the device tier alone, no host tier and no move. The host-tier pull of a request delivered whole stays in `MC_Handoff`; the configurations of entry 3 are kept only in this log.
- **Prediction:** each search completes with no violation of `PlaneSafety` or `ConfigUntouched` within 30 minutes on 16 workers, each under 100 million distinct states. Risk named: `MC_Reuse`, which keeps the tail, both requests and the store, runs past 30 minutes.
- **Outcome:** `MC_Stale` and `MC_Reuse` as predicted; `MC_Switch` INCONCLUSIVE, the risk met by it rather than by `MC_Reuse`. `MC_Stale`: the search completed, 79,305,021 states generated, 12,979,546 distinct, depth 37, no violation, in 4 min 6 s. `MC_Reuse`: completed, 100,122,246 generated, 22,244,987 distinct, depth 43, no violation, in 10 min 14 s. `MC_Switch` was stopped after 21 minutes, to run again on the workers the runs of entry 3 left free: 43,505,259 distinct states at depth 25, 8,853,353 on the queue, no violation.
